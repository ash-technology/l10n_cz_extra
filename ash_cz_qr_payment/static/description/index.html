Czech QR Payment (QR Platba)

Payment QR codes and Czech account numbers on your CZK invoices

Your customers scan the QR code with their Czech banking app and the payment is filled in for them: account, amount, variable symbol, due date and message.

Bank account as IBAN. Enter your company bank account as IBAN, e.g. CZ65 0800 0000 1920 0014 5399: the QR code requires it, and with the Czech format 123-456/0800 the invoice gets no QR code. Your customers still see the familiar account number: the module extends the standard invoice report to print the Czech format derived from the IBAN, e.g. 19-2000145399/0800, with the IBAN on the next line.
Upgrading from version 19.0.1.x? Read the Upgrade notes further below before you upgrade. If you print invoices with a copy of the invoice report (e.g. duplicated in Studio), read Copies of the invoice report too: module updates don't reach the copy.

Key Features

QR Platba on CZK invoices

QR Platba (SPAYD) is the Czech Banking Association standard for payment QR codes, read by Czech banking apps.

The module adds it as a standard Odoo payment QR method, like the SEPA QR code. It appears in the usual place on the invoice PDF and in the customer portal, on unpaid customer invoices in CZK.

Czech account number and IBAN

For a Czech IBAN, the invoice prints the account number in the Czech format, e.g. 19-2000145399/0800, and the IBAN on the next line. Customers who pay by hand can use the format their banking app accepts.

The Czech format is taken from the IBAN according to CNB Decree No. 169/2011 Coll., so both always identify the same account as the QR code.

Complete payment data

The QR code contains the IBAN, the amount due, the due date, the payment reference as the message, the recipient name and the variable symbol made of the digits of the invoice number.

Ready for Odoo 20

The QR method is the same as in the Czech localization of Odoo 20: when you migrate to Odoo 20, uninstall this module. Odoo 20 prints only the IBAN on the invoice; the Czech account number is a feature of this module and disappears with it.

Setup

  1. Enable QR Codes: Invoicing > Configuration > Settings.
  2. Enter your company bank account as IBAN, e.g. CZ65 0800 0000 1920 0014 5399, in Contacts > Configuration > Bank Accounts or in your bank journal (Bank Account Number). The Czech format 123-456/0800 is not supported, but the invoice prints it for you.
  3. Enable Send Money on the account.
  4. With several bank accounts, set the Currency of each. A new invoice gets the first trusted account in its currency (or without a currency) as the Recipient Bank.

The QR code is shown only on unpaid CZK invoices, not on credit notes and paid invoices. QR Platba allows at most 10 digits in the variable symbol, so keep your invoice numbering within 10 digits.

Upgrade notes

Upgrading from version 19.0.1.x

Version 19.0.2.x replaces the module's own QR code with the standard Odoo QR method. Go through the points below before you upgrade. If you can't do it now, stay on 19.0.1.0.0 and don't replace the module files yet; that version stays available as the tag ash_cz_qr_payment-19.0.1.0.0 in the GitHub repository ash-technology/l10n_cz_extra.

19.0.1.x19.0.2.x
Account the first company bank account in CZK (or without a currency); version 19.0.1.x ignored the account on the invoice the account printed on the invoice
Account format the Czech format 123-456/0800 was converted to IBAN by the module must be entered as IBAN; with the Czech format, the invoice gets no QR code. The invoice still prints the Czech format, next to the IBAN

Before you upgrade

  1. Open the company bank account from your bank journal (field Bank Account Number) and replace the number with the IBAN in the same record. Don't create a new account: invoices that already use this account then get the IBAN too.
  2. Enable Send Money on the account.
  3. If the company has several bank accounts, set the Currency of each, so that CZK invoices get the CZK account as the Recipient Bank.
  4. Make sure QR Codes is enabled (Invoicing > Configuration > Settings). Version 19.0.1.x printed the QR code regardless of this setting; without it the invoices get no QR code.
  5. On unpaid invoices created before the upgrade, check the Recipient Bank (Other Info tab): invoices keep the account they were created with.
  6. If you print invoices with a copy of the invoice report, fix it as described in Copies of the invoice report below (cases 1 and 2).

Other changes

  • The QR code follows the QR Codes setting and is printed only on unpaid customer invoices in CZK. Version 19.0.1.x printed it on every CZK invoice, including credit notes, paid invoices and vendor bills.
  • The variable symbol is still made of digits only, but it is now taken from the invoice number instead of the payment reference, and it is not shortened to 10 digits (the same as in Odoo 20). With the default journal setting, where the payment reference is the invoice number, nothing changes.
  • The code also contains the due date, the payment reference as the message (up to 60 characters) and the recipient name.
  • Invoices that were already sent keep their PDF, including the old QR code.

How to upgrade

Replace the module files and upgrade the module right away (Apps > Upgrade): between these two steps, printing invoices fails. Odoo.sh upgrades the module automatically when you push the new version to the repository.

Copies of the invoice report

Module updates don't propagate changes to a copy of the invoice report (e.g. from Odoo Studio, typically account.report_invoice_document_copy_1). The report then has to be edited manually. You can do it in developer mode: Settings > Technical > User Interface > Views, open the report and change its Architecture. Change only the parts described in the following points and check the result by printing an invoice.

1. Copy made with version 19.0.1.x

The copy still contains the old QR code block, and printing fails with 'account.move' object has no attribute 'get_cz_qr_code_base64'. In the architecture:

  1. delete the old QR code block: 
    <t t-set="cz_qr_base64" t-value="o.get_cz_qr_code_base64()"/>

<t t-if="cz_qr_base64">
    <div id="qrcode" class="d-flex mb-3 avoid-page-break-inside">
        <div class="qrcode me-3">
            <img t-att-src="'data:image/png;base64,%s' % cz_qr_base64" style="width:140px;height:140px;"/>
        </div>
        <div class="d-inline text-muted lh-sm fst-italic">
            <p class="fw-bold mb-0" style="color: black;">QR Platba</p>
            <p>Scan this QR Code with<br/>your banking application</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</t>
  2. the following <div id="qrcode" … t-if="o.display_qr_code and o.amount_residual"> is wrapped in <t t-else=""> and </t>: remove these two tags, but keep the div. This div prints the working QR code.

2. Czech account number in a copy

A copy made before you installed this version of the module prints only the IBAN. Replace this part:

<t t-if="o.partner_bank_id">
    <br/> on this account: <span t-field="o.partner_bank_id" class="fw-bold"/>
</t>

with:

<t t-if="o.partner_bank_id">
    <br/> on this account: <span t-if="not o.partner_bank_id._get_cz_acc_number()" t-field="o.partner_bank_id" class="fw-bold"/>
    <t t-else="">
        <span class="fw-bold"><span class="text-nowrap" t-out="o.partner_bank_id._get_cz_acc_number()"/><t t-if="o.partner_bank_id.bank_id"> - <t t-out="o.partner_bank_id.bank_id.name"/></t></span>
        <br/>IBAN: <span class="fw-bold text-nowrap" t-field="o.partner_bank_id.acc_number"/>
    </t>
</t>

The text "on this account:" stays as it is, so its translation is kept.

3. Before you uninstall the module (Odoo 20)

A copy that contains _get_cz_acc_number fails to print once the module is uninstalled. Before you uninstall it, put the original part back: replace the block from case 2 with the first snippet of case 2.

Frequently Asked Questions

The QR code is not appearing on my invoices

Make sure QR Codes is enabled (Invoicing > Configuration > Settings). On the invoice, select CZK QR in the Payment QR-code field (Other Info tab): Odoo then tells you what is missing, e.g. the account is not an IBAN.

Printing fails with "'account.move' object has no attribute 'get_cz_qr_code_base64'"

Your invoice report is a copy made with version 19.0.1.x. See Copies of the invoice report, case 1.

The invoice shows only the IBAN, not the Czech account number

Either the Recipient Bank of the invoice is not a Czech IBAN (starting with CZ), or you print invoices with a copy of the invoice report. See Copies of the invoice report, case 2.

The invoice shows a QR code that opens a web page

That is a different feature: the QR code for paying online via the customer portal (payment providers). It is enabled by the Add QR-code link on PDF setting and does not depend on this module.

Which currencies are supported?

QR Platba codes are generated for invoices in CZK only. Invoices in other currencies use the other QR methods of Odoo, e.g. the SEPA QR code for EUR.
